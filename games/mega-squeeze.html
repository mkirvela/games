Mega Squeeze

A cold-pressed juice empire in one HTML file.

Play in browser Playable

What it is

A clicker. Tap to earn, spend on stronger taps and passive presses, climb ranks, replant for a permanent multiplier, repeat. You know this machine already — the point of the exercise was game feel, so every system in it is built to be felt rather than read off a number.

Squeeze the fruit, fill the glass, buy help, never stop.

What it does with a tap

  • The fruit is jelly. Spring physics, not a scale keyframe. It squashes under the press, pokes away from the exact point your finger landed, and wobbles back with overshoot.
  • The glass is the rank meter. Every squeeze fires a ballistic pour solved to land in the mouth of the tumbler. The surface is a one-dimensional spring water simulation, so each drop lands with a ripple that travels out and reflects off the walls. Fill it and it is SERVED — the glass drains, and a bigger fruit arrives.
  • Frenzy. Mash fast enough and you climb ×2, ×3, ×5, ×8 — with rising pitch, growing screen shake, and a vignette that heats up with the combo.
  • Golden Squeeze. Crits at ×10, with a white flash, sparks and a shockwave.
  • Lifetime spill. A pool along the bottom edge that grows with your total squeezes and never gets cleaned up.

What you build

  • Eight fruit tiers, each hand-built as SVG rather than recoloured: lemon segments, orange pith, blood orange, pomegranate seeds, watermelon stripes, dragonfruit scales, a starfield Cosmic Fig, and finally haloed Ambrosia. The whole palette retints per tier — the fruit, the juice, the panel, the numbers.
  • Eight ranks to earn them, from Rind Rookie up through Zest Sergeant, Nectar Broker, Melon Marshal and Dragon Distiller to Ambrosia Ascendant.
  • Twelve presses in two lines. Five make your own tap heavier — Calloused Thumb, Iron Grip, Zest Grater, Hydraulic Thumb, Thunder Palm. Six squeeze without you — Orchard Kid, Hand Press, Centrifuge, Pulp Cannon, Orbital Squeezer, and a Grove Singularity. The twelfth just buys better crits.
  • Replanting wipes the juice and the grove for a permanent multiplier. Your lifetime squeezes are not part of the deal — that number survives every replant.
  • Offline earnings, capped at two hours, so closing the tab is not punished and leaving it open overnight is not rewarded.

On a phone

The press room collapses into a bottom sheet, so nothing is ever stacked on top of the play area, and a tap anywhere in the stage counts. Add it to the home screen and it runs fullscreen. Two-thumb mashing works — every finger is counted separately.

Where it stands

Finished and playable. One HTML file, no build, no bundler, no dependencies; progress autosaves to localStorage. prefers-reduced-motion is respected throughout, which for a game this loud means most of it switches off.

One asterisk on the house rule: this is the only game here that touches the network, for a Google Fonts stylesheet. It falls back cleanly offline.
