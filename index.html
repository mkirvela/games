The Great Soup

A pot that has simmered since before there was a floor to stand on.

You are the newest hand in the kitchen. Nobody will tell you what is in the pot, and nobody remembers lighting the fire. Your duties are three: keep it fed, keep it moving, keep it hot. Stir the broth, throw things in, and feed whoever sits down at the hatch — then find out how far a soup can go.

02 Casual incremental Balancing

Some of It Bites

A casual incremental about hitting furniture until it screams.

You are a Mimicologist, third class, licensed by the Guild of Ambiguous Furniture. An unhelpful percentage of this dungeon is alive. Hold your hammer on things until they break — about one in five turns out to have a mouth. Every eye in the wallpaper opens while your finger is down, and every open eye gives you less time to catch what you woke.

Mega Squeeze

03 Clicker · Idle Playable

Mega Squeeze

A cold-pressed juice empire in one HTML file.

Squeeze the fruit, fill the glass, buy help, never stop. The fruit is jelly and squashes where your finger lands; every squeeze fires a pour solved to land in the glass, and the juice ripples when it hits. Fill it and a bigger fruit arrives — eight of them, each retinting the whole game around itself.

Pixel Clash

04 Auto-battler · Betting Balancing

Pixel Clash

You do not control the fight. You price it.

Build a warband, let a goblin bookie value it, then bet on your own outcome and watch it burn. He prices what he sees when you arrive — every skill, recruit and dirty trick you buy after he locks the odds is invisible to him. That gap is the whole game.
